AI Products Shipped to Production

Customer-Facing AI, Productized

InsightsAI

A conversational AI assistant for manufacturers that connects directly to ERP and Power BI data, turning static dashboards into natural-language answers. Role-based intelligence for production, finance, operations, and customer service, with anomaly detection and forecasting built in at the Business tier and above. For one trailer manufacturer, that meant a live overlay of weather and supply-chain disruption data on top of their ERP, so production could reschedule before a delay hit rather than after. We took it from concept to a live, tiered subscription product, not a pilot that stayed a pilot. Built on Azure AI with Microsoft Entra governance and role-based access control, and supports Infor VISUAL, Infor SyteLine (CSI), and Acumatica.

Part of the AI and BI portfolio that generated $8 million in new pipeline

Otto Mayshun

An always-on AI consultant living inside the customer support portal, trained on ERP environment data, support history, customer call transcripts, and our solution library. Otto answers system and process questions in plain language and auto-generates a ticket when a human is genuinely needed. We took it from design through production deployment, rolling it out to customers in phases as trust in it grew, and deciding in real time where automation resolves a client's need instantly and where it should route to a human expert instead.

Live 24/7 inside the customer support portal
Implementation AI, ERP Migration

Data Rocket

An AI-assisted Visual-to-Acumatica migration accelerator: an 8-screen workflow that automates extraction, mapping, cleansing, and validation of ERP data, with an embedded AI assistant that detects patterns (null rates, invalid formats, duplicate keys) and suggests targeted cleansing transforms. It replaced inconsistent, per-consultant manual SQL scripts and error-prone Excel mapping with a standardized, auditable process.

Covers the two most common migration tiers end to end
Implementation AI, Production

Code Rocket

An on-premises ERP database scan that inventories customizations, custom tables, triggers, and event handlers. We built the first working scanner, staffed it with an engineering lead and an executive sponsor, and took it through AI Council approval. It turns a line-by-line manual review of multi-thousand-line customization exports into a structured report that maps every finding to a build-or-kill call and drafts the SOW's customization section. Shipped alongside Data Rocket.

Migration costs down roughly 40%
