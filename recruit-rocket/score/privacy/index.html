Free Match Score Privacy Note

Effective November 15, 2026

This note covers the Free Match Score page from Recruit Rocket, run by Brent Blackmon. It is short on purpose.

1. What we keep

Your email address. Whether you ticked the box to get your results and job search tips, and the date you ticked it. The job title you scored against. The location you typed. The date of your score.

We also keep a count of scores per network address for one day, to stop abuse. The address is scrambled first, so we do not keep the address itself. The count is deleted after two days.

2. What we delete

Your resume, your LinkedIn profile and the job posting. We read them to make your score, then they are gone. They are never saved and never written to a log.

3. Who processes your text

Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, processes the text of your resume, your profile and the posting to produce the score. Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy cover that step. Cloudflare runs the page's server and its person check.

4. LinkedIn

Recruit Rocket never logs in to LinkedIn and never reads LinkedIn pages. Your profile comes only from the PDF you upload or the text you paste.

5. Email

If you ticked the box, you may get your results and job search tips by email. If you did not, we do not email you. We never sell or share your email.

6. How to ask for removal

Email brent.blackmon025@gmail.com from the address you used and ask to be removed. Your record is deleted.

7. The score

A Match Score is an estimate from an AI model. It is not a hiring decision and not a promise of an interview. AI can make mistakes.
