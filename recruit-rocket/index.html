What it does

Four jobs a good recruiter does. All of them, every day.

FINDS

Jobs worth your time

Reads your job alerts, the big job boards and the careers pages of companies you name. Skips anything you've already applied to. Grades the rest A, B or C against your resume, pay floor and location.

SPOTS

Companies about to hire

A new CEO, a private equity investment, an acquisition, an executive who just left. Each one comes with the person to contact and a dated source, so you get there before the job is posted.

Paste any posting. Get a Match Score out of 100, what you clearly have, what's missing, and the exact words the posting uses that your resume doesn't. If a job needs something you can't show, it says so before you waste an evening.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once: $29 at launch, $49 after. The only ongoing cost is your own Claude plan.

Will it apply to jobs for me?

No. It finds, scores and drafts. You decide what to send and you send it. That keeps your name on work you've actually read.

Will it make my resume sound like AI wrote it?

It writes from your own resume, in your own wording, and it checks every draft for invented numbers and stock phrases before you see it.

Does it work for students and internships?

Yes. It has an internship mode that tracks application windows and deadlines.

Does it work on a Mac?

It's built and tested on Windows. Mac testing is underway.

Can it guarantee me a job?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who says they can. It gives you better applications and a clear read on where you stand.
