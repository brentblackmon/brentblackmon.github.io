What it does

Four jobs a good marketing team does. All of them, every day.

FINDS

Who's about to move

Reads local business news, state announcements, job postings, county planning and school forecasts, builder news and base relocation offices for the towns and counties you work. Every signal comes with its source link and date.

GRADES

Hot, Warm or Watch

Hot means real demand in the last 90 days and a named person to reach. Warm is real demand with no contact yet. Watch is proposed but not approved. A posting that doesn't actually offer relocation never makes the list.

BUILDS

The whole package

Paste a listing and get the posts, sphere email, flyer, MLS remarks, ad copy and a list of who buys it. Pick a signal and get the email, LinkedIn note and follow-ups dated 4 and 10 days out, written in your voice.

CHECKS

Every draft, before you see it

Fair housing wording, your firm name and license line, an opt-out line on every email, no timing promises, and every number matched to its source. Anything it can't confirm is flagged for you.

The rules it follows

Built so you can trust what goes out under your license.

1

Fair housing comes first

It targets situations, like a job move, a military move or land to build on. Wording like "perfect for families" or "walk to church" gets flagged and rewritten, with a pointer to HUD's guidance.

2

It never makes up a number

Every figure comes from your listing or links to the page that gives that exact number. Anything it couldn't open is marked "confirm before posting."

3

Your firm name on every ad

Your firm name and license line go on every post, flyer and email, exactly as your broker approved them.

4

No digging into private people

It never pulls homeowners from deed, tax or court records. No probate or foreclosure lists, ever.

5

Every email can say stop

Each email draft carries an opt-out line. It never writes cold calls or texts.

6

You send everything

It drafts. It never posts, emails or messages anyone for you, and your files stay in a folder on your computer.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay $99 once. The only ongoing cost is your own Claude plan.

Will it post or send for me?

No. It finds, grades and drafts. You decide what goes out and you send it, so your name is only on work you've read.

Does it make my ads fair housing safe?

It screens every draft for the common problems and points you to HUD's guidance when something is flagged. It's a screen, not legal advice. Your managing broker has the final word.

Does it work outside North Carolina?

Yes. Setup asks which towns and counties to watch, and the daily scan searches there. It was first built and tested in Wilmington, NC.

Does it look up homeowners?

No. It never pulls people from deed, tax or court records, and it never digs up anyone's email or phone. It reaches organizations through named people in public sources, and people only through what they posted themselves.

Does it work on a Mac?

It should run on a Mac in the Claude desktop app, but it hasn't been tested there yet.

Can it guarantee me listings?

No, and be wary of anyone who says they can. It gives you better marketing and an earlier read on who's moving.
